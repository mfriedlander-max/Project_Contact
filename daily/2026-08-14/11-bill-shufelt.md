## Why this decision and not the famous one

The obvious hook is that he left a Steve Cohen hedge fund to hand-bottle beer in a garage. It is
also the single most-asked question in his public life, answered in essentially every interview he
has given. Leading with it would have made this read like every other email in his inbox.

The decision used instead is one he raised himself, unprompted, on the Brewbound podcast on
2026-06-17, where the host moved on without following up: he quit drinking in 2013, and then built
the category leader by explicitly refusing to aim it at people who had also quit. His words, on
the celebrity NA brands now entering:

> "all of them are talking about sobriety, and almost reinforcing a sobriety message for the
> category, which is actually a really small part of the consumers."

The question is a genuine counterfactual about a bet he has never been asked to defend, which is
what `elite-decision-10min` exists to test.

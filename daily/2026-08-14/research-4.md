### The hard decision used in the email

In 2008 SOLA was a scholarship pipeline sending high-performing Afghan students abroad, and it
worked, securing the equivalent of more than $10 million in scholarships. Per the NYT obituary of
Ted Achilles:

> "By 2012, concerned the program was encouraging brain drain, they decided to transform SOLA into
> a boarding high school for girls from across the country, especially those from rural areas."

Obvious choice: keep scaling a funded, functioning programme. Taken choice: shut it down because it
was extracting exactly the people Afghanistan needed, and start over building a physical school
inside a war zone, which then took four more years to open.

### Concrete, sourced

2. Won Best Chef Northwest and Pacific in 2022 on her first nomination. (honolulumagazine.com, 2022-06-13)
3. Built two culinary programs from scratch, the Art Institute of New York City and CUNY
   Kingsborough. (commonground-summit.com, middlebury.edu, hawaiibusiness.com)
4. Sources 75-90% of produce and 90% of meat, dairy and seafood locally. Her own number.
   (hawaiimagazine.com Q&A, 2023-05-09)
5. Pays full employee health insurance, maternity leave, paid vacation and a 401(k) at an
   independent restaurant, and named that as the reason for opening. (hawaiibusiness.com podcast
   2023-05-04; hawaiipublicradio.org 2022-06-16)

### The hard decision

Documented, and used. She left a secure CUNY tenure-track position after fifteen years in New York
to open a restaurant in Honolulu's Chinatown in her forties.

Two things sharpen it. First, she did not want to. Asked by HAWAIʻI Magazine (2023-05-09) whether
opening a restaurant in Hawaii was always the dream, the answer was no; her husband wanted to
relocate and she could have stayed. Second, she left the profession with humane hours for the one
without them and then rebuilt it to have them. From her Beard acceptance speech: "We wanted to open
up a restaurant, the restaurant that I wanted to work in. I wanted to give more money to our
workers, I wanted to provide maternity leave, I wanted to give health insurance to everyone."

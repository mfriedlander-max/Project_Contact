## What this is

The cold open for people who have no reason to care that you go to Middlebury. Leads with
*student founder building an AI startup*, peer framing, founder to founder, and drops the
school entirely.

## Hypothesis

Against `cold-all-15min`: a founder reads "student founder building an AI startup" as someone
doing the same thing they did, while "current Sophomore at Middlebury" reads as a student
asking for a favor. Also drops the ask from 15 to 10 minutes, which is a smaller thing to say
yes to.

Two changes at once, so a win here doesn't tell you which one did the work. If it beats the
retired variant, split it: hold the framing, test 10 vs 15.

## Body

```
Hi {first_name},

My name is Max. I'm 20 years old and a student founder building an AI startup. {insert}

{curiosity_question} If you had 10 minutes to chat, I am hungry to learn. I promise I'll pay it forward!

Best,
Max
```

## Slots

- `{insert}`: one sentence on what *they* did, specific enough that it could not be sent to
  anyone else. Goes in the **Personalized Insert** column.
- `{curiosity_question}`: what you genuinely want to know from this person. The real payload;
  everything above it is credentials.

## Subject line

Default is above. Roughly a quarter of sends used a bespoke subject mirroring the target:
`20 Year Old Founder to 22 Year Old Billionaire`, `Middlebury Sophomore at OpenAI`. Those are
a real variable and were never tracked separately. If you want to know whether bespoke subjects
beat the default, that needs its own id.

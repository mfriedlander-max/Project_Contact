## What this is

The warm version, for when someone has given Max permission to use their name. Taken from a
real email to Parker Harris via Ernie Parizeau.

Only use this when the referral is **real**. Naming someone who did not offer is the fastest
way to burn two relationships at once.

## Why it asks for more time

15 minutes, not 10. A referral already cleared the trust hurdle that the 10-minute ask is
designed to lower, so there is no reason to under-ask.

## Reference email

Em dashes are banned (`email_personalization_prompt.md`), so the two in the original are split
into separate sentences here. Nothing else is changed.

```
Hi Parker,

Ernie Parizeau suggested I reach out. I'm a sophomore at Middlebury studying applied math and building software on the side.

You were an English lit major who taught yourself to code and co-founded one of the most important software companies in the world. I'm doing the same now with AI, trying to move fast and build quality software, not just accepting what the model says.

I'd love 15 minutes to chat. I'm hungry to learn, ambitious, and curious about entrepreneurship. I promise I'll pay it forward.

Best,
Max
```

## Structure

1. `Hi {first_name},`
2. **The referral, first line.** `{Referrer} suggested I reach out` - it is the strongest thing
   in the email, so nothing precedes it.
3. **Who Max is**, folded onto the same line.
4. **The parallel.** Not just what they did, but the line connecting their path to his. Parker
   taught himself to code from a humanities degree; Max is teaching himself to build with AI.
5. **The ask** plus the three-word posture: hungry to learn, ambitious, curious.
6. `I promise I'll pay it forward.`
7. `Best,` / `Max`

## The parallel is the hard part

This variant lives or dies on step 4. It requires finding the specific thing in someone's
history that rhymes with what Max is doing now. A generic parallel ("you're an entrepreneur, I
want to be one") is worse than none, because it announces that no research happened.

If no honest parallel exists, use `cold-midd-personal-10min` with the referral line prepended
instead of forcing one.

## Slots

- `{referrer}` - **must** be a real person who agreed. Record who in the sheet's **Source**
  column as `referral: {name}`.
- `{their_path}` - the specific, verifiable thing they did.
- `{the_parallel}` - what Max is doing that rhymes with it.
